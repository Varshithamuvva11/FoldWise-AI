Data Preprocessing & Zero-Leakage Pipeline Architecture

Strict Scikit-learn Pipeline isolation: Scalers, imputers, and encoders are fit exclusively inside training folds to eliminate data leakage.

Zero Data Leakage Enforced
Methodological Rigor Preventing Evaluation Bias
Why Preprocessing Leakage Ruins Machine Learning Models

When numerical features are standardized using parameters ($\mu, \sigma$) computed across the entire dataset before splitting, information from the test fold leaks into the training process. This artificially reduces test error, masks overfitting, and produces models that fail catastrophically in deployment.

FoldWise AI Guarantee: Every transformer is bound inside a sklearn.compose.ColumnTransformer and sklearn.pipeline.Pipeline. When cross-validation runs, fit() is called only on the training indices of that fold; the validation fold is evaluated strictly with transform().

pipeline = Pipeline([
