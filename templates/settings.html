Research Project Overview
Project Title: Cross-validation Strategy Comparison (K-Fold, Stratified, Nested, Time-Series) and Its Effect on Reported Performance and Model Selection.
Core Research Thesis: Evaluation methodology directly shapes reported model performance. Applying inappropriate validation schemes introduces severe statistical hazards: optimistic selection bias in naive hyperparameter tuning, class dropout in imbalanced datasets, and catastrophic lookahead leakage in sequential time-series forecasting.
Zero-Leakage Guarantee: All transformations (StandardScaler, SimpleImputer, OneHotEncoder) are encapsulated within Scikit-Learn Pipeline objects and fitted strictly within training fold indices. Validation and test folds remain completely unobserved during preprocessing and fitting.

5-Step Live Script
Step 1: Automated Validation Strategy Diagnosis

Navigate to Cross-Validation Lab. Select breast_cancer and run the Strategy Advisor. Observe how an imbalance ratio of 1.68:1 automatically triggers Stratified K-Fold and flags Standard K-Fold as hazardous.

Step 2: Empirical Stratification Contrast

In the Benchmarking Studio, execute Benchmark Strategies on Breast Cancer. Show that Stratified K-Fold maintains identical class ratios in all validation partitions.

Step 3: Quantifying Model Selection Bias with Nested CV

Demonstrate double-loop Nested Cross-Validation. Point out how non-nested tuning reports an optimistic score, while Nested CV measures genuine generalization error.

Step 4: Proving Lookahead Leakage on Time-Series Data

Select air_passengers in Benchmarking. Compare Time-Series CV against Random K-Fold. Show that random shuffling artificially depresses RMSE by leaking future trends into past folds.

Step 5: Multi-Model Comparison & 360° Radar Visualization

Open Model Comparison, select 5 classifiers on Breast Cancer, and click Execute. Present the dynamic rankings, stability standard deviations, and the 6-axis 360° radar chart.
